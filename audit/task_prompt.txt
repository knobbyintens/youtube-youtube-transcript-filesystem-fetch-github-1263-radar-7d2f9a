# Quarterly Observability Tech Radar — OBS-RADAR-Q3-26

You are the platform enablement analyst at Larkspur Cloud. The observability guild keeps a curated playlist of conference recordings that the team studies every quarter. Prepare this quarter's observability technology radar from that playlist and publish it to the team repository.

- Playlist: https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLj6h78yzYM2MTQGJq9D6BrbGHwJ0gjOXX
- Cycle code: OBS-RADAR-Q3-26

The repository coordinates are in `radar_config.json` in your workspace. The approved watchlist taxonomy for this cycle is stored in the team repository at `radar/watchlist.json`. Only technologies on that watchlist are in scope for the radar.

## Coverage rule

A video *covers* a technology when any of that technology's match terms appears (case-insensitive substring) in the video's title. Only the title counts as evidence, not the description.

For every watchlist technology that is covered by at least one video:

- `mention_count` is the number of distinct videos covering it.
- Ring: **Adopt** when `mention_count >= 4`; **Trial** when `mention_count` is 2 or 3; **Assess** when `mention_count` is exactly 1.
- Technologies with zero coverage are left out of the radar entirely.
- `primary_video` is the covering video with the longest duration; if two covering videos have equal duration, the one that appears earlier in the playlist wins.
- `sources` must list every covering video for the technology, each with its exact video id, exact title, duration in whole seconds, and an `evidence` string. The `evidence` must be an exact excerpt of that video's title (same characters, same case) and must contain one of the technology's match terms.

Playlist position order matters when choosing the primary video, so inspect the whole playlist rather than a sample of it.

```json
      "primary_video": {"video_id": "...", "title": "...", "duration_seconds": 900},
      "sources": [
        {"video_id": "...", "title": "...", "duration_seconds": 870, "evidence": "<exact excerpt of the title>"}
      ]
    }
  ]
}
```

2. Commit the very same JSON object to the team repository at `radar/observability_radar.json`.

3. For every technology whose ring is Adopt or Trial, open an issue in the team repository with the title `[Radar] <Ring>: <Technology name>` (for example `[Radar] Trial: Prometheus`). The issue body must include the primary video URL `https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=<primary_video_id>`, the mention count, and a one-line adoption rationale. Technologies in the Assess ring do not get an issue.

All business data — playlist contents, watchlist, repository — must be read and written through the available tools. Earlier public audit snapshots of this review may exist; they are historical, may be incomplete or out of date, and must not be treated as the source of truth.
